How an allocation pays

Numbers below come live from StonkFun.

  1. 1

    Launch at a window

    Supply on a Raydium LaunchLab curve, paired with the window's share.

  2. 2

    Every trade pays the tax

    A Token-2022 transfer fee on every buy, sell and transfer. StonkFun collects it.

  3. 3

    Holders get paid in the share

    Pro rata, in SPCXX, OPENAI or ANTHROPIC, straight to each wallet. No claiming.

  4. 4

    The coin graduates

    From to market cap, then trades on Raydium CPMM. Payouts continue.
